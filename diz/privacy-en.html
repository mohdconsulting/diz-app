Privacy policy – Diz (دز)

Last updated: October 2026. Data controller: MOHD Consulting AB – contact: mohamad@mohdconsulting.se.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, phone number, password (stored hashed), role (customer or provider) and the kinds of jobs a provider accepts.
  • Requests: service, description, addresses, price, status and offers.
  • Messages between customer and provider about a job.
  • Precise location: shared only by the provider after a job has been assigned and until they arrive, and only while the app is open, so the customer can follow them. The position is deleted on arrival.
  • Ratings and comments customers leave about providers.
  • Payment records (amount and status) when payments are enabled.

How we use it

Only to run the service: matching customers with providers, completing jobs and payments, and preventing abuse. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps.

Who can see it

Providers see open requests that fit them. The customer and the assigned provider see the details about each other that are needed for the job. The app administrator can read jobs and conversations for moderation and dispute resolution. Data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete my account). Your account data, open requests and messages are then deleted. Completed jobs and payment records are kept anonymised (without your name and number) for bookkeeping.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us at the address above.
